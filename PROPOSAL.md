# LK Agro Market Project Proposal

## Section 1.5 Proposed Solution

* **Crop Price Suggestion**: When a farmer creates a listing, the system automatically calculates a suggested selling price based on the average price of active and recently accepted listings of the same crop type within the same district over the previous 60 days. If fewer than three local listings are available, the system falls back to a national average across all regions. The suggestion is displayed as a recommended reference price and the farmer may accept or override it freely.

---

## Section 3.1.1 — Product Listing and Crop Management

* **Price Recommendation System**: The system shall provide a crop price suggestion to farmers when creating a new listing. The suggestion shall be calculated as the average price per unit of the same crop type from active and accepted listings in the farmer's district within the last 60 days. Where fewer than three district-level listings exist, a national average shall be used as the fallback. The suggestion is non-binding and the farmer may enter any price.
